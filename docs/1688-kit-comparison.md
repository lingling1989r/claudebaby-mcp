# 1688 MCP sourcing: compare complete ESP32 kits with Amazon and overseas offers

A cheap ESP32 board and an assembled voice-assistant kit are different products. A useful comparison starts with the selected SKU, included parts, order quantity, currency and shipping assumptions. A search result alone is not a complete bill of materials or a supplier-quality assessment.

## Start with a comparable specification

Define the required chip family, board, display, microphone/speaker, power supply, case and wiring. Separate a basic ESP32-C3 experiment from a complete voice device that may need more memory, audio hardware or assembly. A budget is a target to test, not a verified market price.

## Method

1. Discover current 1688 and target-marketplace tools and required parameters.
2. Search “ESP32 kit”, complete development kits and the required chip family. Make a small candidate list.
3. Open each available product detail and variant record. Verify the selected SKU, included parts, pack size, MOQ and tier price.
4. Compare with Amazon, Temu or Walmart only where data is available. Match function and packaging before comparing prices.
5. Record missing shipping, taxes, certification, assembly and after-sales information. Do not substitute the listing's minimum price for the full kit price.
6. Prepare a shortlist and questions for the supplier. Purchase and supplier communication are separate steps.
