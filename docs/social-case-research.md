# Xiaohongshu and YouTube MCP workflows for brand and creative research

Collect social examples around a question: how did the brand demonstrate value, what did users ask, and which creative mechanism could another brand adapt? Query each currently available platform, then retain source evidence. Data retrieval and creative interpretation are separate steps.

## Method

1. Verify the platform and search/detail/comment capabilities in the actual tool catalogue.
2. Search brand names, category terms and use cases. Record whether the result is official, creator-made, sponsored or a concept piece.
3. Keep original links, dates, text and available engagement metrics. Exclude pure promotional claims without a usable process or evidence.
4. Attach only available original images, cover images or permitted screenshots. Label them correctly; don't imply full-video analysis from a cover.
5. Explain the mechanism: hook, problem, demonstration, visual device, audience and next action.
6. Write adaptation variables: brand, product benefit, scene, constraint and validation question.

## AI creative ads

Published creator prompts can be recorded with their source. If the prompt is not published, label your reconstruction as an inferred draft. Never attribute an invented prompt to the original creator, or call a brand concept film an official campaign.

## B2B acquisition

Look for concrete trust-building actions, demos, case explanations and real inquiry evidence. A large view count is a content signal, not proof of acquired customers. Separate audience fit, content execution and verified commercial outcome.
