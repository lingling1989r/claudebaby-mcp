# Research requests to adapt

These are proposed workflows, not completed datasets. Start small, discover actual tools and report unsupported capabilities. Replace the brand, geography and time window as needed.

## B2B acquisition

Research how companies in our category build trust and generate inquiries through currently supported social platforms. Retain source links, distinguish process evidence from claimed outcomes, exclude pure promotional posts and recommend three small tests for our audience. Do not attribute sales to views.

## Reddit customer needs

Study real Reddit posts and comments about ESP32 voice assistants. Start with ten candidates, record search queries and dates, and separate original statements, grouped needs and your recommendations. Keep excluded promotional candidates in a review log.

## Brand launch history

Trace a brand's earliest publicly found overseas activity. Include official accounts, users, media and moderators. Separate pre-launch and first-year clues, preserve publication dates and label earliest found rather than absolutely earliest. Distinguish self-reported campaign effects from validated outcomes.

## Competitor reviews and complaints

Read actual product or social comments for our competitor using available tools. Preserve source excerpts, dates and identifiers; separate complaints, context, inferred needs and proposed product fixes. State sample limits.

## 1688 supply candidates

Find complete ESP32 kits rather than bare boards. Verify selected SKU, included parts, chip family, MOQ and quantity-based prices. Retain supplier/product links, unknown fields and questions for supplier verification. Do not endorse quality from a listing alone.

## Overseas product comparison

Compare available 1688, Amazon, Temu and Walmart offers by model, function, pack size and currency. Mark missing shipping, taxes, assembly and support costs. Separate evidence from pricing hypotheses and do not call retail minus sourcing price profit.

## Neutral public-issue listening

Over a defined period, summarize public policy statements and service feedback from supported public social sources. Separate institutions, media and users, preserve sources and uncertainty, and do not treat online activity as a representative opinion poll.

## AI creative references

Find official brand ads and creator concept pieces on supported platforms. Label original image, cover or screenshot, explain the creative mechanism and adaptation variables, and separate published original prompts from inferred prompt drafts.
